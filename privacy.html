Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy describes how the Red Canvas desktop app ("the app") handles information.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to your YouTube account and YouTube Analytics for the channels you own (channel details, video statistics and reports).

How the data is used

The data is used only to display analytics for your own channels inside the app on your computer.

Storage and sharing

All data and access tokens are stored locally on your computer. The app does not send your data to any server operated by the developer, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data.
